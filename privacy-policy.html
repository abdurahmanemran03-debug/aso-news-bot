ASO NEWS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

ASO NEWS is a news publishing application designed to collect, process, and publish news content automatically to the ASO NEWS Facebook Page.

2. Information We Use

The application may use information provided by Meta's Facebook Platform, including Page-related information and access permissions required to publish content to the ASO NEWS Facebook Page.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the Meta Platform is used only for operating the ASO NEWS publishing system, including publishing news posts and managing Page content.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information and access credentials used by the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the ASO NEWS publishing system and maintain its functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

ASO NEWS may use third-party services such as Meta Platforms and Google services to provide automated news processing and publishing functionality. Their respective privacy policies may also apply.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the ASO NEWS administrator through the official ASO NEWS Facebook Page.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
